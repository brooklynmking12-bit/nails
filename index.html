Custom press-ons

Made for your hands

Press-ons that fit like they grew there

Most press-on kits give you ten generic sizes and hope two of them work. We size each nail individually from a measurement kit we mail you, then paint the set by hand in the studio.

The result sits flush at the cuticle, doesn't catch on your hair, and comes off without damaging your natural nail.

How custom sets work

Three steps, about ten days

1

Measure

We mail you a free sizing kit — or stop by the studio and we'll measure in two minutes.

2

Design

Choose length, shape, and finish. Send a reference photo, or let us design something for you.

3

Wear

Your set arrives with adhesive tabs, glue, a buffer, and instructions. Reuse it as many times as you like.
